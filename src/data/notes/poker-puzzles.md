# Definitions 
**"The nuts"**: the best possible hand in any given scenario. Preflop, the nuts are AA. On a board with 865 rainbow, 97 are the nuts. 

**Community cards**: the 5 cards on the board

**Hole cards**: the 2 cards each player has

# Puzzle 1: Lowest nuts
What configuration of community cards and hole cards gives you the lowest possible nutted hand on the river? 

[Answer](https://www.param.me/notes/poker-puzzles-1-answer)

# Puzzle 2
What configuration of 5 board cards and 2 hole cards make it such that: 
1. Looking back, you know that you had the best possible hand on the flop
2. *and* the turn
3. On the river, you are guaranteed to lose the pot to any other hand -- not even a chop 

The answer is unique (up to suits)
